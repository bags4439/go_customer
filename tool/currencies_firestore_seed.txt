Currencies collection — one-time seed (CA_COUNTRY_02_PROFILE_COMPLETION)
========================================================================

Collection path: currencies

Composite index (create when the Console prompts, or under Indexes):
  Collection: currencies
  Fields: countryCodes — Arrays, isActive — Ascending

The client query uses array-contains on countryCodes plus isActive == true.
